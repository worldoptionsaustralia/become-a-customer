Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open an account with World Options?

It's free to open a World Options account and start using our online portal. You only pay for your shipments.

Can I use multiple carriers?

Yes — that's why the online portal was created, so you can compare rates between different carriers and delivery times and choose what's best for your business and budget.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No — there are no contracts or fixed-term agreements. You can open an account and start comparing rates with no commitment.

Will I get training on how to use the World Options shipping portal?

Yes — every new business customer is assigned an Account Manager or International Shipping Consultant who'll walk you through the portal and is on hand for any questions afterwards.

Will I get an account manager who I can speak to regularly?

Yes — you'll have a dedicated Account Manager or Shipping Consultant to talk through your account, portal changes, and the best shipping options for your business.

Can I get the same rates and service by going directly through the shipping carrier?

Unlikely — we negotiate bulk rates with major carriers that low-volume businesses can't access on their own, plus you get our Customer Service Support to save you time and hassle.

Do World Options operate carrier deliveries themselves?

No — we don't physically deliver your goods. Our portal lets you compare and book with multiple carriers, who handle the physical shipping for you.

My shipping requirements vary each month — is World Options suitable for this?

Yes — whether you ship one large batch a month or several small shipments throughout, the portal is built to flex with your volume and needs.
